How it works

Pointsman is a small service that takes decisions for a city’s apps. The AI only answers questions. Public data gives facts about the place. The city’s own rules decide. People have the last word.

  1. 1 · The reportA resident’s app saves it
  2. 2 · Passed onThe data platform tells Pointsman
  3. 3 · PointsmanQuestions, facts, rules
  4. 4 · The resultSaved back, open to every app

Why cities use it this way
  • The AI only answers fixed questions. It never decides alone.
  • Facts come from public data, so anyone can check them.
  • The city writes the rules, and can read every decision later.
  • A person has the last word whenever it matters.
